Malaysia's "Ancient Hidden National Treasure" is making its way back to the health world - the Tiger Milk Mushroom - also known as Lignosus Rhinocerus and Cendawan Susu Harimau. Although Tiger Milk Mushrooms have been used for more than 400 years by Traditional Malay & Chinese medicine practitioners and the orang asli community, it has recently taken the holistic health world by storm due to its powerful healing properties on more than a dozen of common medical conditions, especially respiratory and lung health issues.
